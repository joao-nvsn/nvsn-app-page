This app keeps your Shopify inventory automatically matched to your real supplier stock — no spreadsheets, no manual updates.

Active — syncing on schedule

How it works

Sync frequency Every 4 hours
What it updates Inventory quantities only
Matched by Product barcode

What this app does

  • Downloads the latest stock levels from your supplier feed(s)
  • Matches each item to your store's products by barcode
  • Updates the on-hand quantity for anything that matches
  • Leaves everything else in your store untouched

What this app doesn't do

  • Doesn't change prices, titles, descriptions, or images
  • Doesn't create or delete products
  • Doesn't touch orders, customers, or discounts
  • Doesn't zero out stock for products it can't match
